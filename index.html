Linear Equations in One Variable

Class 9 Math · Chapter 2 Notes

NCERT PDF

Core Concept: An equation of the form ax + b = c where a ≠ 0 and x is the variable.

  • Transpose Rule: Moving terms across the '=' sign flips operations (+ to -, × to ÷).
  • Cross Multiplication: If (ax + b) / (cx + d) = k, then ax + b = k(cx + d).
  • Solution Verification: Substitute calculated x back into LHS and RHS to verify equality.

Linear Equations in Two Variables

Class 9 Math · Chapter 4 Notes

NCERT PDF

Standard Form: ax + by + c = 0 where a, b, c are real numbers and a, b ≠ 0.

  • Number of Solutions: A linear equation in two variables has infinitely many solutions.
  • Graph: Every point (x, y) on the line represents a valid solution pair.
  • Axes Equations: Equation of x-axis is y = 0; Equation of y-axis is x = 0.

Surface Areas & Volumes (Cones & Spheres)

Class 9 Math · Chapter 11 (CBSE 2026-27)

NCERT PDF

Key Formula Sheet:

Right Circular Cone: • Slant Height (l) = √(r² + h²)
• Curved Surface Area = πrl
• Total Surface Area = πr(l + r)
• Volume = (1/3)πr²h
Sphere & Hemisphere: • Surface Area of Sphere = 4πr²
• Volume of Sphere = (4/3)πr³
• CSA of Hemisphere = 2πr²
• TSA of Hemisphere = 3πr²
